Smoking Habit

Smoking is a primary risk factor for respiratory, gastrointestinal, and systemic malignancies.

Alcohol Consumption

High alcohol intake produces carcinogenic metabolites that elevate risk in liver and digestive organs.

Red Meat Intake

Frequent consumption of red and processed meats is correlated with increased colorectal cancer incidence.

Pollution Exposure

Chronic inhalation of PM2.5 and industrial air pollutants induces persistent cellular oxidative stress.

Autoimmune Disorders

Systemic inflammatory disorders alter immune surveillance and may contribute to elevated risk profiles.

Diabetes History

Metabolic dysfunction and chronic hyperinsulinemia correlate with elevated cellular proliferation risks.

Physical Activity

Regular moderate-to-vigorous exercise improves metabolic health, immune surveillance, and hormonal balance.

Antioxidant Nutrition

Cruciferous and leafy vegetables provide essential dietary phytochemicals, antioxidants, and protective fiber.

Sleep Duration

Adequate circadian rest (7-8 hours) is vital for DNA repair mechanisms and immune system homeostasis.

Stress & Well-being

Chronic psychological distress elevates systemic cortisol levels, impacting sleep and cardiovascular and immune regulation.
