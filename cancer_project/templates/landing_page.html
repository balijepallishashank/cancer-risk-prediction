Dr. Nanda Rajaneesh

An eminent Laparoscopic Surgeon and Surgical Oncologist, is the Founder and Chairman of the Society for Prevention of Cancer - SPOC.

At SPOC, Dr. Nanda leads a team of dynamic young medical experts and talented individuals from other walks of life who volunteer their time and energy towards this noble cause.

Dr. Nanda Rajaneesh is a GI Laparoscopic, breast, bariatric surgeon and surgical oncologist currently working at Apollo Spectra hospital, Koramangala, Bangalore and Sakra World hospital, Bangalore. She is the Founder director and chairman of SPOC (Society for Prevention of Cancer). She has done her MBBS from JJM Medical college, Davangere, MS from Mysore Medical College. She holds Fellowship in G.I. She was the founding member of the team at Nova that pioneered the concept of short stay surgeries. As a leader, she is extensively involved in treating breast cancer and bringing awareness on cancer prevention. She has given many corporate lectures, published numerous papers, and authored books including 'Life with Fibromyalgia' and 'Risk Factors in Breast Cancer'.

Her work focuses on preventive healthcare and lifestyle risk awareness to reduce cancer risks and recurrence. Graduate of Harvard University Surgical Leadership Program.

Vision of the Project

The Cancer Prevention Guide is a visionary project aimed at providing comprehensive resources and actionable insights to empower individuals in their journey towards reducing cancer risk. With a focus on education and awareness, this guide endeavors to equip people with the knowledge and tools necessary to make informed lifestyle choices.

Our Approach

The Cancer Prevention Guide project integrates evidence-based risk factor evaluation into an accessible digital framework. By analyzing lifestyle, nutritional, environmental, and personal history indicators, the project helps individuals understand risk distributions and take proactive steps towards healthier living.

Why Cancer Risk Assessment?

Global Health Burden: Cancer is one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Early awareness, proactive screening, and lifestyle modifications can dramatically improve health outcomes.

Preventive Healthcare: Many cancer types are influenced by lifestyle factors and environmental exposures. Understanding risk factors empowers individuals to make proactive health choices.

Personalized Insights: Every individual's lifestyle profile is unique. Lifestyle assessment provides tailored educational guidance for personalized preventive adjustments.

Awareness and Education: This tool serves as an educational platform to encourage regular medical check-ups and adherence to age-appropriate clinical screening protocols.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is lifestyle cancer risk assessment?
- Lifestyle risk assessment evaluates observable environmental, metabolic, and behavioral factors associated with elevated health risks, offering guidance for preventive modification.
How does lifestyle affect cancer risk?
- Lifestyle habits such as smoking, alcohol consumption, high red meat intake, and physical inactivity have well-documented correlations with cellular stress and carcinogenic processes. Healthy habits help mitigate these risks.
Can this assessment replace a medical diagnosis?
- No. This tool is purely for public health education and awareness. It is not a clinical test or diagnostic screening. Always consult a qualified oncologist or healthcare professional for clinical advice.
Is genetic testing important?
- For individuals with significant family histories of specific cancers (e.g., BRCA mutations in breast/ovarian cancer), formal clinical genetic counseling is strongly recommended.
How should I use these results?
- Use your result as a guide to identify opportunities for positive lifestyle changes and to initiate informed conversations with your doctor regarding routine health screenings.
